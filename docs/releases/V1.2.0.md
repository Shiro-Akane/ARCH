# ARCH v1.2.0

Chinese translation: [V1.2.0.zh-CN.md](V1.2.0.zh-CN.md).

This update introduces self-gravity as a domain-wide physical field and reorganizes
its shared CPU/CUDA execution path. It also separates the Driver's runtime, stage,
and I/O responsibilities, retires obsolete configuration and EOS paths, and makes
low-density state handling explicit. The release tag identifies the published
source snapshot; this note alone does not publish a package.

## Physics and numerical methods

- **Composite AMR self-gravity.** A shared matrix-free Poisson operator and
  multigrid solver use the active AMR hierarchy. Each required Hydro stage solves
  from its current density generation and publishes potential and face forces;
  momentum and energy use the shared gravity source mathematics. Gravity is not
  routed through the burning ODE's KLU or cuDSS provider.
- **Native geometry on CPU and CUDA.** Validated configurations include Cartesian
  periodic domains in 1D–3D and isolated 3D, isolated radial cylindrical and
  spherical 1D, and tested full-azimuth cylindrical/spherical 2D/3D domains,
  including origin, axis and pole joins. Dynamic AMR, restarts and representative
  Hydro, burning and thermal-diffusion coupling are covered for these tested
  configurations; [GravityBox](../../simulation/GravityBox/README.md) and
  [SNIaCoupled](../../simulation/SNIaCoupled/README.md) provide reusable inputs.
  The latter is a C/O hotspot example, not a complete white-dwarf explosion.
- **Low-density robustness.** Physical density and specific-energy bounds use
  existing `sml_rho` and `min_eint/max_eint` controls. Accepted repairs record
  their state changes; invalid EOS states and unresolved thermal energy fail
  explicitly. Flux limiting and CFL bounds were tightened, so prior step counts
  and timings are not a numerical baseline for this version.

## Source and interface changes

- Driver work is grouped by runtime, stage, scheduling, dispatch and I/O role;
  AMR, CUDA, gravity and numerical sources have clearer subdirectory ownership.
  CPU and CUDA continue to share the physical formulas and solver control, with
  backend-specific storage, kernels and completion handling.
- Case code includes `<UserInterface.h>` and `<GlobalDefs.h>` through the ARCH
  build contract. The Core configuration schema, inspection and bounded CPU
  preview interfaces support GUI clients; inspection is not a promise that a
  simulation can run. The Studio front end is maintained separately.
- Strict free-energy 3D/4D table EOS remains supported. Obsolete normalized
  direct-table representations, permissive EOS fallbacks and unused gravity
  interfaces were retired. Shared EOS and network work was reduced without
  changing the documented physical acceptance rules.

## Migration from v1.1.0

- Remove the retired input keys `enforce_mass_conservation`,
  `burn_verbose_level`, `ode_use_numerical_jac`, `ode_freeze_jacobian` and
  `timeintegrator`. They now produce `RETIRED_PARAMETER`; the registered
  `dt_init`, `dt_min` and `tstep_change_factor` keys retain their active roles.
  Revisit low-density floors and EOS table provenance for existing cases. See
  the [P1.5 migration record](../development/P1_5ImplementationReport.zh-CN.md).
- **Checkpoint format 6 cannot read v5.** It records state-control and repair
  identities alongside the gravity policy and controls. Potential and force are
  reconstructed from the saved density on restart; CPU and CUDA use the same
  format. Finish old runs with their original executable or start a new v1.2.0
  run from appropriate initial data. Reconfigure and rebuild the source after
  updating because internal headers and target ownership changed.
- ARCH inputs and outputs remain in CGS, including IdealGas and the Core/GUI
  configuration contract. The [case guide](../guides/SimulationCase.md) lists
  units and case-authoring conventions.

## Verification and scope

The [gravity validation index](../../validation/gravity/README.md) links independent
Poisson and force checks, composite AMR convergence, source/energy budgets,
restart, curved-coordinate seams and CPU/CUDA comparisons. The
[low-density record](../../validation/low_density/results/p1_5-20260922/README.md)
covers the changed floor and rejection behavior. Representative four-module
runs and local GPU acceleration are recorded with their specific source,
inputs and hardware; they do not qualify every policy combination or guarantee
GPU speedup for all sizes.

Self-gravity does not yet support partial-azimuth curved domains, external mass
sources or a Jeans AMR indicator. Helmholtz stellar transport currently supplies
thermal conduction, not viscosity or species diffusion. The recoverable trial
failure contract for tabular burning/NSE remains under review. Consult the
[method-combination rules](../Reference.md#combining-methods-and-physics) and
[known limitations](../Reference.md#known-limitations) before changing the
validated setup.
